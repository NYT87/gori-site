FAQ

Pricing questions

Is the self-hosted edition really free?{{icon.plus}}

Yes. Gori is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only. You can run, study, and change it at no cost. You pay only for your own hosting and any provider fees.

What does the AGPL mean for my company?{{icon.plus}}

Using Gori unmodified, including from closed-source agents that call its API, adds no obligations to your code. If you modify Gori and let others use it over a network, you must offer them your modified source. Ask your counsel for your specific case.

Who pays for Naver and Kakao API usage?{{icon.plus}}

You call providers with your own application and credentials, so each provider bills your account. Gori adds nothing on top of provider fees.

When will Gori Cloud launch?{{icon.plus}}

There is no date yet. The developer API comes first, then the dashboard. Watch the repository or email us and we'll let you know.

Do I need a Gori account to self-host?{{icon.plus}}

No. A self-hosted deployment never contacts a Gori service. Your provider credentials and data stay on your infrastructure.
